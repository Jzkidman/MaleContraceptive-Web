1. WHAT IS THE MALE BIRTH CONTROL PILL?

A new hormone-free male contraceptive pill, YCT-529, has passed early human safety trials with no reported side effects and works by blocking a vitamin A receptor essential for sperm production.

The pill has been making headlines and has sparked controversial topics about the pill itself as previous trials had been discontinued due to "side effect being adverse", many of which are common to the female contraceptive pill.
